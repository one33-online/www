What we do

The divisions that run through South Africa were not settled in 1994. They are alive, present, and felt by people right now — across race, class, culture, gender, and generation. One33 exists inside that reality and works to bridge it.

01 — Relational

We sit with what is broken.

One33 facilitates reconciliatory dialogue — between and within communities divided by race, class, culture, and generation. Not as outside observers. As participants in the same fractured country.

This work cannot be rushed. It is built on presence, trust, and the willingness to stay when the conversation becomes uncomfortable.

Direct Facilitation
02 — Practical

We connect what restores.

One33 identifies what communities have lost — not what we think they need — then connects the organisations, government resources, and skill sets that can restore it.

We do not arrive with solutions. We broker the relationships that produce them.

Connector · Facilitator
03 — Distinct

We ask before we act.

Most organisations prescribe solutions before they understand the need. One33 reverses this. We begin by asking what has been lost — then work backwards to build the response.

This is the difference between service that sustains dependency and work that actually restores dignity.
