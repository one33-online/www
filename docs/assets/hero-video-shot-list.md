## Hero Video Shot List (with actual references called out)

### Shot 1 – Aerial inequality confrontation shot

**Purpose:** Formal city and township/informal area in one visual idea – the geography of inequality.

Primary reference sources (all yours):

- [Johannesburg – Unequal Scenes](https://unequalscenes.com/johannesburg)  
  Aerials showing mine dumps, hostels, Kya Sands vs Bloubosrand, Alexandra vs Sandton, townships like Tembisa and Vusimuzi.

- [Durban – Unequal Scenes](https://unequalscenes.com/durban)  
  Aerials of Papwa Sewgolum Golf Course vs informal settlement, Morningside wealth, Cornubia, vulnerable low‑lying communities.

- [Cape Town – Unequal Scenes](https://unequalscenes.com/cape-town)  
  Aerial contrasts: Masiphumelele / Lake Michelle, Imizamo Yethu / Hout Bay, Cape Flats, Dunoon/Wolwerivier, Stellenbosch / Kayamandi, Strand / Nomzamo.

- [Strand / Nomzamo – YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLfJGL-83n0)  
  Video of a township and suburb split by a wide, empty buffer strip – classic physical separation image.

- [Imizamo Yethu / Hout Bay – YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JK8y1bGs1Hw)  
  Video of dense hillside township pressed directly against a leafy, affluent estate.

- [Pexels – Aerial informal settlement photo](https://www.pexels.com/photo/aerial-view-of-south-african-informal-settlement-)  
  Free stock photo of a South African informal settlement from above: dense zinc roofs, organic streets.

You can cut these so that:
- One beat is a “line” or “buffer” (Strand/Nomzamo, Masiphumelele / Lake Michelle).
- One beat is “side‑by‑side” (Alex vs Sandton, Imizamo Yethu / Hout Bay).
- One beat is “tapestry” – the informal‑settlement aerial from Pexels as a more abstract texture of inequality.
