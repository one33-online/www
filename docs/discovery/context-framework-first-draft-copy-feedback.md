## Language to Revisit

These constructions drift toward generic nonprofit register. Suggested alternatives below.

---

**"skilled facilitators and community practitioners"**

Too institutional. It describes the role without conveying the weight of the work.

> Try: *"people who are trained to hold difficult conversations"* or simply *"the people who do this work"* — concrete and human rather than credentialed.

---

**"systemic brokenness"**

Academic language. It distances the reader from what is actually meant.

> Try: *"what was built to keep people apart"* or *"the structures that made division the default"* — harder, more specific, harder to nod past.

---

**"long-term reconciliation processes (not quick fixes)"**

The parenthetical undercuts the line. "Not quick fixes" reads as defensive.

> Try: *"reconciliation that stays"* or *"the kind of presence that can't be parachuted in"* — makes the same point without the bracketed disclaimer.

---

**"meaningful, ongoing engagement"**

A phrase that could belong to any nonprofit anywhere.

> Try: *"being present long enough to be trusted"* or just *"showing up, consistently"* — grounds the abstraction in something real.
