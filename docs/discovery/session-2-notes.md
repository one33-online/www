# Discovery Session 2 — Design Review Notes

---

## Overall Reaction to Concepts

Richard responded positively to all four concepts — liked the clarity and simplicity across the board. Key word: *spacious*. He doesn't want more; he wants the important things to be important.

Black and white palette with the earthy/terracotta accent is confirmed. He prefers it over the blue variant. The accent colour rationale (soil, blood in the earth) landed well.

---

## Concept Direction

**Concept A is the foundation.** Richard chose it for the hero — specifically for its clarity and directness. The hero copy reads as a statement, not a sentence. The font plays a role here: he found the serif (Cormorant) slightly soft — reads as flowing rather than declarative. Worth revisiting whether the display weight is punchy enough.

**No background video in the hero.** He likes it static. The reasoning: a video invites interpretation shaped by personal history; the text as written leaves no room for misreading. He values the lack of ambiguity.

**Pull elements from other concepts into A:**

- From Concept C: the three-part layout for "Why We Exist" — *"Restoring what was broken. Rebuilding what matters. Living in unity."* — displayed with prominence, side by side rather than stacked vertically.
- From Concept D: section-level statements. Each section should carry a single bold statement that communicates the point even if nothing else is read. He noticed their absence in A. Examples he called out: *"We don't just repair relationships, we rebuild communities"* and *"Partner with us — reconciliation takes time, takes presence, takes trust."*
- From Concept D: the Partner section layout — three options (give, volunteer, connect) displayed side by side, not vertically stacked.
- From Concept D: the governance/documentation section — *"All legal documentation is in place"* with the NPO, PBO, and Section 18A clearly accessible. Location is flexible (body or footer), but it should be present.

---

## Copy Notes

Richard engages closely with words. A few specific callouts:

- *"Give financially, offer your skills, open your relationships"* — he likes this framing.
- *"Reconciliation takes time. It takes presence. It takes trust."* — confirmed strong, keep it.
- *"We don't just repair relationships, we rebuild communities."* — confirmed, keep it.
- The copy in Concept D resonated most: *"Most organisations arrive with a solution. One33 exists at the intersection of relational and practical because without both, neither lasts."*
- He flagged the double-up in the Partner section — having both a section header and a subline doing the same job. One or the other, not both.

## Mobile

Flagged that 60–70% of visitors will likely be on mobile. Richard acknowledged this as important. No specific direction given, but it should inform layout decisions — particularly the side-by-side treatments he favoured, which will need to stack gracefully.
