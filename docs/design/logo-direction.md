# One33 — Logo & Brand Mark

## Current State

Logo designed by **Sade**. Black and white. No formal brand guide exists yet — colours, extended typography, and favicon are all outstanding.

---

## Wordmark

- Name renders as **One33** or **ONE33** — not the numeral "133"
- Richard likes the written-out form with the numerals
- Ubuntu-style feel — this quality should be preserved in any adaptation

### Fonts in Use (confirmed by Sade)

| Element | Font |
|---|---|
| "One" text | Plus Jakarta Sans |
| Tagline | Montserrat |
| "33" numerals | Apricots |

The Apricots numerals are where the logo's personality lives — a decorative, warm display font against the cleaner geometric "One." The pairing carries warmth and character that the rest of the brand should acknowledge, particularly in accent colour and display type choices.

## Implications for Site Typography

The logo contains no serif. Heavy editorial serif display typography on the site (e.g. Cormorant Garamond at large sizes) creates contrast with the mark rather than extension. This isn't necessarily wrong, but should be intentional. If a display serif is introduced, **Instrument Serif** is the safer call over Cormorant given the logo's geometric sans base.

Plus Jakarta Sans (used in the wordmark) and **Instrument Sans** (chosen as the site UI baseline) are sufficiently close in character — both modern geometric sans families — that they will sit comfortably alongside each other in the interface.
