## Brand Character

One33 is a South African reconciliation ministry operating at the intersection of relational restoration and practical justice. The brand must carry the weight of that mission without softening it.

**The feeling:** Confrontational without aggression. Strong, just, ordered. There is space for the broken thing, but the brand does not dwell there — it points toward resolution. Not a charity. Not a startup. Something more like a conviction.

**What it is not:** Friendly, approachable, tech-adjacent, generic nonprofit, growth-signalling, sentimental.

**Three words:** Emotional. Aggrieved. Grateful. (confirmed by Richard, Session 3, 18 June 2026)

> Note: the original working words were *Resolved. Grounded. Human.* — placeholders pending Richard's input. In Session 3 he answered the three-word exercise with how he wants people to *feel*, replacing the earlier brand-character words. The deeper anchor behind these three is **unlocked** (see [../discovery/session-3-notes.md](../discovery/session-3-notes.md)).

## Visual Register

The closest analogues are serious institutional brands — Equal Justice Initiative, Open Society Foundations, Apartheid Museum — where typography carries authority and the mark is restrained. Not the community-app or social-enterprise visual language.

- Spacious layouts, one thought at a time
- Image-driven over typographic on the site
- South African story, not globalised
- Bold but not loud
